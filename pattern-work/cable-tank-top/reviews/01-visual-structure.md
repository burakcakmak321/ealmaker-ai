# 01 – Visual & Structure Review: Cable Tank Top (M & L)

Sources checked: all 12 page renders (p-01…p-12), cover photo img-000, schematic img-001, motif charts img-003, full panel chart img-005, text.txt.
Confidence scale: **certain** / **likely** / **cannot tell**.

## About the photo (img-000)
- It is the only product image. It looks like a rendered or heavily staged image: the light is perfectly even, the garment is perfectly symmetrical, it hangs flat without gravity distortion, and the rope cables have soft scalloped edges with no readable crossings (**likely** rendered).
- So it cannot prove that a sample was knitted from these instructions. Treat it as a design reference, not as evidence.
- There is no scale reference, so all comparisons below are ratios only.
- Not visible: the back (apart from a little WS fabric seen through the neck), the seams, the inside and the band bind-offs.

## Summary of observed design (photo)
- Knitted, not crochet (**certain**). Sleeveless vest/tank with a round crew neck, a ribbed hem, a ribbed neckband and ribbed armhole bands (**certain**). Ribs are consistent with k2,p2 but this cannot be confirmed (**cannot tell** k1p1 vs k2p2).
- Side seams are probably present but hidden; front and back are probably separate pieces (**cannot tell** from the photo; the text says flat pieces, seamed).
- Front motif order, left to right: **seed | narrow rope | diamond chain | narrow rope | honeycomb | narrow rope | diamond chain | narrow rope | seed** (**likely**). Raised single knit columns also edge several motifs.
- Diamonds form a continuous chain: the strands cross in an X between diamonds. The **diamond interiors are filled with seed/moss texture**, and the background outside the diamonds is reverse stockinette (**likely**).
- The honeycomb is the widest motif, about 4–5 cells across, and it runs up to the neckband (**likely**).
- Rope cable crossing direction cannot be read: the image resolution and rendering do not show the crossings (**cannot tell**). Whether the left and right ropes mirror each other is also unknown (**cannot tell**).
- Proportions (front view, ratios):
  - Hem rib ≈ 10% of body length.
  - Armhole depth ≈ 36% of length.
  - Neck opening (outer band edge) ≈ 45–50% of chest width.
  - Each strap ≈ 18% of chest width.
  - About 7–8 diamond repeats between the rib and the shoulder.

## What the instructions produce (for comparison)
- Two flat pieces, worked from the hem up and seamed. Hem rib is 2x2 over 12 rows (≈ 5 cm ≈ 9% of length).
- Panel order: **seed | p3 | Rope 6 | p2 | Diamond 10 | p2 | Honeycomb 8 | p2 | Diamond 10 | p2 | Rope 6 | p3 | seed**. There is one rope per side, outside the diamonds only.
- Diamond interiors are reverse stockinette (purl dots). The diamonds link through the C4F on row 15 and the k4 on row 1.
- Honeycomb 8 (8 sts) is narrower than each Diamond 10 (10 sts). It produces about two cell columns, which reads more like an OXO-style cable than an all-over honeycomb.
- Both ropes are C6F, so they twist the same way (not mirrored).
- Armholes:
  - BO 4 + BO 4.
  - 4 decreases on alternate rows (≈ 4 cm of shaping).
  - Then straight to the shoulder.
  - Each side loses 8 of 80 sts (10%). Depth is 49 rows ≈ 20.4 cm ≈ 36% of length. This matches the photo.
- Shoulders: horizontal bind-off of 18 sts (M) or 22 sts (L), with no shoulder slope.
- Front neck:
  - Flat bind-off of 20 sts.
  - 4 single decreases each side over 8 rows.
  - Then about 16 straight rows.
  - Result: a U shape with vertical upper sides. Depth is 25 rows ≈ 10.4 cm. Width at the top is 28 sts (35% of piece width).
- Back neck: flat bind-off of 28 sts, then 8 straight rows. This gives a shallow rectangular notch about 3.3 cm deep.
- Cable repeats from row 13 to the shoulder: 123 rows ÷ 16 ≈ 7.7. This agrees with the photo.

## Open questions for the designer/user
1. Which is the target: the photo design (4 ropes, wide honeycomb, seed-filled diamonds, wide crew neck) or the written 54-st panel? Is the cover image a real knitted sample or a render? If it is a render, should it be captioned "illustrative"?
2. Should the two Rope 6 cables mirror each other (C6F one side, C6B the other), or both twist the same way as written?
3. Is a U-shaped front neck with vertical upper sides intended, or a rounder crew? The latter would need more gradual decreases.
4. Is the back neck meant to be a straight bind-off with only 8 rows of depth, as written, or shaped as the schematic semicircle suggests?
5. Should seed stitch be used inside the diamonds? That would change the chart.
